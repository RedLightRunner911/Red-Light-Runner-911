Police Radar – Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026.

Police Radar is free. It has no accounts, no subscriptions, no ads, no analytics and no crowd sharing. It listens passively for Bluetooth signals that nearby equipment already broadcasts. It never connects to, pairs with or writes to any device.

What stays on your phone

  • Bluetooth scan results, matched police-gear detections and your settings are kept on your phone.
  • Device IDs are scrambled with a random value that changes every scan session.
  • Nothing you detect is uploaded or shared by the app.

Location and Bluetooth

  • Location is used to show your position on the map and to warn about cameras ahead, while the app is open or while a scan you started is running.
  • Scanning starts only when you tap Scan. It keeps running while you use other apps, with a notification showing it is on, and stops when you tap Stop, tap Stop in the notification, or close the app.
  • The app does not ask for “allow all the time” location access.

Services the map uses

To draw the map and search for places, the app requests map tiles and search results from third-party services (OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap tiles, Photon address search, and Mapbox for map previews and navigation). These requests include the map area you are viewing, and for search or navigation the text you type or the route you request. These services see your IP address like any website would. Their own privacy policies apply.

Recordings you choose to share

If you record a session and tap Share, the file goes only where you send it, using your own apps.

Children

Police Radar is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the app publisher listed on the app's store page.
