## Full description

Patrol Radar is a public-awareness map. It listens passively for Bluetooth Low Energy signals that equipment such as body cameras, in-car systems, and fixed license-plate cameras already broadcast in public, and shows likely matches on a coarse map.

How it works
• Passive only. Patrol Radar never connects to, pairs with, or sends anything to another device.
• Coarse by design. Detections are snapped to a 150–250 m grid, never an exact position.
• Private on the device. Device IDs are hashed with a salt that changes every session. MAC addresses and serial numbers are not kept. Your precise location is shown on your screen. It is not added to the crowd map.
• Optional shared map, off by default. If you turn it on, only the grid cell, a 5-minute time slot, the gear category, and a confidence level are shared, plus a random session id that resets when you turn sharing off. Shared reports are delayed 12–17 minutes and expire after about 2 hours.
• Background scanning shows a persistent notification and stops when you tap Stop, or after 6 hours.
• Optional City Chat uses an anonymous account (a random id, no email or password). Messages are deleted after 24 hours. You can delete your own messages sooner.
• Optional Navigation Mode gives turn-by-turn driving guidance. Place search and routes are sent to the navigation providers named in the privacy policy. Do not interact with the phone while you are driving. A passenger can use it, or pull over first. Follow the law and the directions of public-safety personnel.
• No ads and no analytics SDK.

Patrol Radar Pro
• $1.99 per month in the United States, billed by Google Play. Other countries show the price Google Play displays.
• When Google Play offers it on your account, the monthly plan includes a 2-week free trial (offer id free-trial-10d). You are not charged until the trial ends. Cancel in Google Play before then and you are not billed.
• Pro unlocks traffic-camera map pins and speed, red-light, and traffic-monitoring alerts, full Stop History shading and year, time-of-day, and weekday filters, and historical activity-zone notices.
• School-zone and work-zone alerts stay free. Stop History shading is included with Pro.
• Pro is verified with Google Play. An anonymous account id is used for that check and for City Chat. There is no email sign-up.
• Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

Limitations
Bluetooth range is about 100–150 m. Matches are probabilistic, and many devices broadcast nothing. Do not rely on the app for safety decisions. Historical stop shading is public records, not a live police location.
